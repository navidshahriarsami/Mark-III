# MARK III — Vision

## The Vision

MARK III is the AI that lives with you. Not in the cloud. Not on a server. On your own hardware, learning from you, always present, always private.

## What MARK Is

**An AI operating system.** Not an assistant that responds to queries. An intelligence that understands its owner's context, patterns, and goals — and acts accordingly.

## Core Beliefs

- **Your intelligence should be yours.** MARK runs on your hardware. Your data never leaves unless you send it.
- **AI should know you.** Generic AI is useless. MARK personalizes to each owner from day one.
- **Intelligence should be everywhere.** One brain, every device. Desktop, Mac, iPhone, Watch — MARK is always there.
- **Latency is respect.** MARK responds in milliseconds for common queries. It never wastes your time.

## The Ecosystem

```
You (anywhere)
    ↓
MARK Mobile (iPhone) — voice, GPS, emergency layer
MARK Companion (Mac) — relay, notifications, Samantha
MARK Core (Desktop) — permanent brain, always-on
MARK Watch (future) — presence, quick replies
MARK Box (future) — dedicated hardware, plug-and-play
```

## Commercial Vision

Every MARK installation becomes a unique AI that knows its owner. It is not a generic assistant — it is your personal operating system.

Revenue model:
- MARK Box: ~$150-200 dedicated hardware
- MARK Connect: ~$6-12/month relay service (replaces Tailscale)
- Enterprise: MARK for teams, shared knowledge bases

## Why This Matters

The world's most powerful AI systems are inaccessible to most people — they require technical setup, cloud subscriptions, and privacy concessions. MARK changes that: powerful, private, personal, and completely owned by its user.
